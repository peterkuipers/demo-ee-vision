```
PSD for all trees

The Process Structure Diagram (PSD) contains a compact tree structure on the left side and on the right side the disks of the transaction kind are stretched so the response links and wait links between the coordination acts and facts can be shown.
If nothing is revealed from the narrative the standard structure is response link from the (TK01/pm) to the [TK02/rq] and a wait link between the (TK02/ac) and the [TK01/ex].
The compact tree structure and the transaction kind disks are so aligned, the actor roles on the left side cover the responsible areas of the transaction kinds on the right side.
The product kinds are placed on the transaction kind disk, so the split the order and the result phase. The coordination acts and facts are placed at the right side of the product kind accordingly.
The name of the transaction kind is displayed above or below the transaction kind disk on the right side.
```
